Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 6, 2026

The Endo Initiative is committed to respecting your privacy. This website is designed to collect as little information as possible while remaining secure and functional.

1. Information We Collect

This website does not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, accounts, uploads, or user-submitted content.

The only information collected comes through Cloudflare Web Analytics, which provides anonymous traffic statistics without identifying individual visitors.

Anonymous information may include:

  • Approximate page views
  • General device or browser information
  • Region-level geographic information
  • Referring websites, when applicable

Cloudflare Analytics does not store personally identifiable information for these measurements.

2. Cookies and Tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, cross-site tracking technologies, or behavioral profiling systems.

Cloudflare may temporarily process technical information for security purposes, such as bot detection and abuse prevention.

3. How Information Is Used

Any anonymous information collected is used solely to:

  • Understand general traffic patterns
  • Improve website functionality and usability
  • Detect malicious traffic and security threats

No personal profiling, targeted advertising, or sale of information occurs.

4. Third-Party Services

The Endo Initiative relies on Cloudflare for hosting, security, and anonymous analytics. This may involve:

  • Temporary processing of IP information for security purposes
  • DDoS protection and firewall services
  • Anonymous traffic measurement

We do not sell, share, or monetize visitor information.

5. External Links

This website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy, security, or content of external sites.

6. Data Security

This website benefits from Cloudflare security services, including encryption, firewall protection, and automated threat mitigation.

No personal data is intentionally stored by the Endo Initiative through this website.

7. Children's Privacy

This website is not specifically directed toward children and does not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page along with an updated revision date.
