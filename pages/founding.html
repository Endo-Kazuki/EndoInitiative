Hey, thanks for stopping by.

This is Endo writing to you.

I wanted to share a short story about why this organization exists and why I'm here.

Life for me was anything but easy. For most of my life, I dealt with struggles, pain, and experiences that left me believing things would never get better.

To my own surprise, they eventually did.

For the first time in a very long while, I reached a point where I could actually breathe. During that time, one simple question came to mind:

"Who do I want to be?"

My entire life had been spent surviving. I had never really stopped to ask myself who I wanted to become.

The answer didn't come immediately. It took months of reflection, sorting through my experiences, and trying to understand what kind of person I wanted to be.

Eventually, I arrived at a simple conclusion:

I want to be the person that I never had for myself growing up, the person that I so desperately needed, and then to be that person for others.

A thought that plays in my head often is wishing I could have been there for my younger self. I know his life would have turned out differently.

But while I can't do that for myself, I can do it for others.

So I'm trying my best to.

And from that idea, the Endo Initiative was born.

Because no one should walk alone.

The systems we live with were built by people, and because of that, they can be improved by people, same as you or I.

At its core, this initiative is guided by a simple belief:

Your burdens are my burdens, and my strength is your strength.

I see the struggles people face. I see the flaws in the systems around us. And I want to spend my life using whatever strength, knowledge, and resources I have to help carry those burdens alongside others.

Change won't happen in a single night.

But I'd gladly spend every night for the rest of my life helping ensure that it's on its way.

Signing off with love,

— Endo

Living for Today in Service of Tomorrow
